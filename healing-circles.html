What is a Healing Circle?

How often have we said to someone we love: I will think of you, I will pray for you, I will meditate for you, and then wondered how to actually do it?

A Healing Circle offers a simple way. People who wish to help gather together and are guided to create a meditative, healing atmosphere. People in need of healing, from whatever that might be, join the circle and allow that healing energy to flow over and through them.

Givers

You come to help create the healing atmosphere. No prior experience is needed, although if you meditate, so much the better. Givers are filled with this healing energy before it radiates out to others, so many find they receive a real boost themselves.

Receivers

You come for your own healing. There is nothing you need to do other than relax, be receptive and allow the healing energy in. You are warmly welcome, whatever has brought you here.

How it works

Each circle runs for forty minutes.

We teach you how to give or receive this healing energy: a skill you can then use at any time to send healing to anyone you care for.

The method is simple, traditional and well tested. It is completely inclusive, with no religious connotations.

You are welcome to invite people you know and care for to join you.
