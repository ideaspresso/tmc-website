About us

A community built on four decades of teaching

The Meditation Community grew from the work of Dr Ian Gawler OAM — a pioneer of meditation in Australia who has taught Mindfulness-based Stillness Meditation since 1981, and trained meditation teachers since 1988.

In 2025, Ian and Daniel Traini founded The Meditation Community to bring that lineage online: a warm, welcoming home where anyone can learn, practice and grow — with live sessions led by senior teachers, recordings for whenever life gets in the way, and the Healing Circle for those meditating through illness. What makes us different is not an app or an algorithm. It is people, practicing together.
